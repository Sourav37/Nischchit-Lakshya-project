THE FOUNDATION OF DISCIPLINE

WHAT IS NISCHCHIT LAKSHYA?

Nischchit Lakshya (নিশ্চিত লক্ষ্য) হল একটি structured 5-day trading workshop যেখানে participants-দের trading-এর গুরুত্বপূর্ণ concepts, timing, market analysis, strategy, risk management এবং disciplined execution সম্পর্কে step-by-step understanding তৈরি করা হবে।

The ultimate goal of this workshop is NOT to promise instant profits or unrealistic returns. The goal is to empower you with clarity:

01

What You Are Doing

Understand underlying mechanics and market structure before entering any trade.

02

Why You Are Doing It

Base every decision on concrete data, logic, and proven concepts rather than emotions.

03

When You Should Act

Master precise market timing, candle behaviors, and time cycle windows for maximum clarity.

04

How To Manage Risk

Protect your hard-earned capital today so you can capitalize on opportunities tomorrow.

COURSE CURRICULUM

৫ দিনের structured learning journey for aspirants & traders

DAY 0 / SESSION 01

PRAVESH

(INTRO & FOUNDATION)
  • Introduction to Trading

  • Key Terminologies Explained
  • Broker & Capital Management

ট্রেডিং-এর basic foundation, stock market-এর মূল ধারণা, market structure এবং গুরুত্বপূর্ণ terminology সহজ ভাষায় শেখানো হবে।

DAY 1 / SESSION 02

KAAL CHAKRA

(TIMING CANDLE FOR EVERYDAY, EXPIRY DAY, SWING & POSITIONAL)
  • Daily Timing Candles
  • Expiry Special Timing Candles
  • Swing & Positional Timing Candles
  • Time Cycle Mastery & Entry/Exit Clarity

Timing-এর গুরুত্ব বোঝানো হবে এবং daily, expiry, swing ও positional trading-এর context-এ timing candles নিয়ে বিস্তারিত আলোচনা করা হবে।

DAY 2 / SESSION 03

DHARMA RANGE TOOL

(OUR LEADING INDICATOR FOR INTRADAY, SWING, POSITIONAL)

Dharma Range Tool-এর application বিভিন্ন trading approach-এর ক্ষেত্রে কীভাবে structured ভাবে ব্যবহার করা হয় তা শেখানো হবে।

DAY 3 / SESSION 04

LAKSHYA BHED

(TECHNICALITY’S & FUNDAMENTALS WITH CONCEPT AND STRATEGY)

Technical analysis, fundamental analysis এবং strategy-building concepts নিয়ে deeper understanding তৈরি করা হবে।
